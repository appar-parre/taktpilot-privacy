TaktPilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

TaktPilot is an Android application for Lean production, daily management, problem solving, standards and learning.

This Privacy Policy explains how TaktPilot accesses and handles information on your device.

1. Developer and contact

TaktPilot is developed and published by Andreas Parment.

Privacy contact: appar@parre.se

2. No account required

TaktPilot does not require a user account to use its core functionality.

3. Information handled by the app

TaktPilot is designed as a local-first application. Information that you create in the app is stored locally on your device.

Depending on which features you choose to use, this may include:

  • notes and text entered into Gemba Walks, deviations, problem-solving records, Kaizen or PDCA activities, audits, KPI records, SOPs and other user-created content;
  • photos that you choose to capture or attach;
  • location information when you explicitly use a feature that adds location or geotag information;
  • locally generated settings and app configuration.

TaktPilot uses this information only to provide the functions you request inside the app.

4. Camera access

TaktPilot may request access to the camera when you choose to take a photo for a record, SOP, deviation or other supported feature.

Camera images are used for the feature you selected and are not transmitted to the developer by TaktPilot.

5. Location access

Some TaktPilot features can optionally use your device location, for example to add location information to a Gemba record.

Location access is used only when required by the feature you choose to use. TaktPilot does not transmit your location to the developer.

You can deny or revoke location permission through Android settings. Features that do not require location remain usable.

6. Photos, media and exported files

When you choose to save or export a photo or other supported content to your device, TaktPilot may use Android system media functions to write that file to device storage or the device gallery.

Files deliberately saved outside TaktPilot remain under your control and may remain on the device after TaktPilot is uninstalled.

7. Data collected by the developer

This version of TaktPilot does not intentionally collect or transmit user-created app content, photos, location data or other personal data to the developer.

TaktPilot does not use advertising or third-party tracking for its core functionality.

Important: this statement assumes the published release does not include an analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party SDK that transmits user or device data. If such a service is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before release.

8. Data sharing

TaktPilot does not sell user data.

TaktPilot does not share user-created app data with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

If you deliberately share or export information using Android sharing functions, the destination and handling of that information are controlled by you and the receiving application or service.

9. Storage and retention

App records are stored locally on your device and remain there until you delete them, clear TaktPilot app data or uninstall the app, subject to Android storage behavior.

Files that you deliberately save to the device gallery or other external storage locations may need to be deleted separately.

Your Android device, Google account, device manufacturer or backup provider may provide device backup or restore functions independently of TaktPilot. Those services are controlled by your device and account settings and are subject to the relevant provider's privacy terms.

10. Security

TaktPilot is designed to minimize external data transfer by keeping its core user data local to the device.

No electronic storage method is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for using appropriate device security such as a screen lock.

11. Children's privacy

TaktPilot is designed as a professional productivity and production-management application and is not directed to children under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if TaktPilot's features or data practices change. The current version will be made available at the privacy-policy URL shown in Google Play and from within the app.
