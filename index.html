Privacy Policy — Takt Apps Suite

Effective date: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to TaktPilot, TaktBoard and other productivity, learning and production-management apps developed and published by Andreas Parment that link to this policy (the “apps”). Features vary between apps. Sections describing optional features apply only where those features are available and you choose to use them.

1. Developer and contact

The apps are developed and published by Andreas Parment. For privacy questions, contact appar@parre.se and specify which app you use.

2. Accounts and local operation

The apps do not require a user account for their core functionality. App records and settings are stored locally on your device. The apps do not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization.

3. Information handled by the apps

Depending on the app and the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • Content you enter, such as notes, tasks, observations, improvement activities, standards, audit results and learning records.
  • Production and planning information, such as board configuration, line and product names, shifts, targets, counts, timings, stoppages and performance records.
  • Photos or other supported files that you choose to capture, select or import.
  • Location information that you explicitly choose to attach to a record.
  • Text produced through optional system speech recognition.
  • Settings, preferences and locally generated identifiers used to organize records or identify a board.

This information is used to provide the functions you request, including displaying, calculating, saving, restoring and exporting your records. The apps do not automatically send this content to the developer.

4. Optional permissions and system services

Camera and photos. Where supported, an app may use the camera or Android's system picker when you choose to take or attach a photo or scan a code. Selected content is used for that feature. The apps do not transmit your photos to the developer.

Location. Where supported, location is accessed when you explicitly choose a feature that attaches your location to a record and grant the necessary permission. The apps do not track your location in the background or transmit it to the developer.

Speech recognition. Where supported, you may choose Android's speech-recognition service to enter text. The service configured on your device may process audio remotely under its provider's privacy terms. The resulting text is handled like text you type into the app. The developer does not receive your voice recordings through the apps.

You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings. Functions that do not depend on those permissions remain available.

5. Collection, advertising and Google Code Scanner

The apps do not automatically transmit your app records, photos or saved location information to the developer. They do not contain advertising or developer-operated analytics or crash reporting. Technical data processed by Google Code Scanner / ML Kit is described below.

Google Code Scanner / ML Kit in TaktPilot

TaktPilot uses Google Code Scanner through Google Play services for optional QR-code scanning, with auto-zoom enabled. Image processing takes place on your device. Google states that the scanner does not store the scanned images or scan results. The scan result is returned to TaktPilot to provide the function you request.

The SDK sends technical information to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. This can include device and app information, device or installation identifiers, performance measurements, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature versions, usage events and error codes. With auto-zoom enabled, additional data includes a generated scanning-session identifier, changes in zoom level and predicted coordinates of a bounding box that may contain a barcode.

Google uses these metrics to measure performance, debug, maintain and improve its APIs, and detect misuse or abuse. According to Google's ML Kit disclosure, this technical data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred to third parties. Google Play services may also download or update the scanner module. QR scanning is optional; core functions that do not depend on it remain available without using the scanner.

For details, see Google's ML Kit data disclosure, ML Kit terms and privacy information, Google Code Scanner documentation and Google's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive this SDK telemetry or control Google's retention of it.

Android, Google Play and your device manufacturer may separately process diagnostic, store or system-service information under their own settings and privacy policies.

6. Import, export, sharing and backups

Where supported, you can import files, export records, create or restore backup files, or share content through Android's file picker and sharing functions.

You choose the file, destination and receiving app or service. A selected destination may be local storage or a cloud provider. That provider's terms govern its handling of the information. The apps do not sell user data or automatically share app records with advertisers or data brokers.

Android backup and device-transfer behavior varies by app, Android version, device and settings. Where enabled, the operating system or backup provider may copy app data to a backup service or another device. These copies are controlled by your device and account settings and the provider's privacy terms; they are not developer-operated cloud synchronization.

7. Storage, retention and deletion

Local app data is retained until you remove it using available app controls, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app, subject to Android storage and restore behavior.

Exports, shared files, gallery images and manually created backups can remain after the app is uninstalled. Delete those copies separately from the locations and services where you saved or shared them. System backups may have separate retention and deletion controls, and restoring a backup may restore previously removed data.

The developer cannot access or remotely delete records stored only on your device or in your own backups.

8. Privacy enquiries

If you contact the developer, the information you choose to send, including your email address and message, is used to respond to and handle your enquiry. Email is processed by the email services used to deliver and manage that correspondence. Please avoid including sensitive app records unless needed for your request.

Correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You may use the contact address above to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have sent, subject to applicable law.

9. Security

The apps use Android's private app storage for their internal records and settings. Access to files you deliberately export or share depends on the destination you choose. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep exported records and backups in a suitable location. No electronic storage or transfer method is completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

The apps are intended for professional productivity, learning and production management and are not directed to children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the covered apps' features or data practices change. New data practices will be described before they take effect, with additional notice and consent where required. The current policy is available through the privacy-policy link provided in each covered app and its store listing.
